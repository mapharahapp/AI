## 1. Topik yang dipilih

**Utusan Kesultanan Kutai yang dikirim menjemput dua "orang buntut".** Sumber utama: ulasan Alfred Russel Wallace atas buku Carl Bock, *Nature*, 3 November 1881 (arsip Charles H. Smith, Western Kentucky University). Wallace menulis bahwa Bock dikirim Gubernur Jenderal Hindia Belanda dari Sumatra ke Kalimantan. Di Kutai, "Tjiropon, an old and faithful servant of the Sultan of Koti, declared, in the Sultan's presence, that he himself had seen some of these people in the Passir country." Ekornya "from two to four inches long", dan "the people cut holes in the floor to receive it, so that they could sit down comfortably!" Bock "thought this so absurd that he disbelieved the whole story, but the Sultan of Koti was greatly impressed by it", lalu "it was decided to despatch Tjiropon on an embassy to the Sultan of Passir" dengan surat yang meminta dikirimi dua "Orang-bontoet". Tjiropon kembali "very crestfallen" dan "had not been able to procure any tailed men."

Atas permintaan Bock, Residen Banjarmasin lalu mengirim rombongan dan surat ke Paser. Jawabannya: "the Sultan's personal attendants are known by the term 'Orang-boentoet di Sultan di Passir'", Sultan Paser "had never heard of any other", dan ia sudah "ordered the messenger to depart instantly on pain of being flogged". Tjiropon tetap bersikeras: "I have seen the Orang-bontoet long ago, and have spoken to them." Wallace: "Almost the only amusing episode in the book."

Good News From Indonesia (3 Sep 2020, mengutip National Geographic Indonesia) menambahkan: perjalanan Bock ke pedalaman Kalimantan Timur dan Selatan tahun 1879, misinya atas titah Gubernur Jenderal Johan van Lansberge, dan Bock menjanjikan **500 gulden** kepada sang abdi kalau berhasil membawa orang berekor ke Kutai. Dua detail ini tidak ada di ulasan Wallace, jadi hanya masuk caption dengan atribusi.

Alurnya: Kesultanan Kutai pernah mengutus abdinya menjemput dua manusia berekor... dan utusannya malah diancam dicambuk (0–5,8 dtk) → abdi tua itu mengaku pernah melihat mereka di Paser, katanya sampai melubangi lantai supaya bisa duduk → penjelajah Eropa tak percaya, tapi sultan terkesan, abdi itu diutus membawa surat → diusir, pulang tangan kosong → ternyata di Paser, "orang buntut" cuma sebutan untuk pengiring pribadi sultan → CTA follow.
