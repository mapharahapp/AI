# Naskah (verbatim from the doc "Titik Heran: Naskah & Storyboard Video #1")

## Voice over (5 paragraphs, read as-is)
Ini bukan kepala mumi. Ini lentera lobak, nenek moyang labu Halloween.
Irlandia, sekitar tahun 1900. Tiap malam Halloween, orang mengukir lobak jadi wajah seram, lalu menyalakan lilin di dalamnya, untuk mengusir arwah.
Masalahnya, tradisi ini memudar. Seorang guru sampai menyerahkan satu lentera ke museum, karena sudah tak ada yang membuatnya.
Tapi tradisinya nggak mati. Imigran Irlandia sudah membawanya ke Amerika, lalu mengganti lobak dengan labu yang jauh lebih gampang diukir. Cuma pindah benua... dan ganti sayur.
Kirim ke teman yang paling penakut, lalu lihat reaksinya.

Recording notes: Halloween read "he-lo-win"; hook firm and fast; background slower, half-whisper; resolution rises, relieved, small pause before "dan ganti sayur".

## Storyboard (9:16, ~30 s target; timecodes shift with the recorded VO)
1. Hook 0:00-0:04 — extreme close-up of a turnip lantern face, candle flickering inside; push toward the eyes, small zoom jolt on "lobak". Text: "INI BUKAN KEPALA MUMI" then "INI LOBAK". Audio: low hum + match strike at 0, dark music fades in.
2. Latar 0:04-0:07 — dark world map, the red Titik Heran dot drops on Ireland, zoom to the island. Text: "IRLANDIA, SEKITAR 1900". Audio: "ting" when the dot lands, night wind.

4. Masalah 0:12-0:18 — sepia archive look; teacher handing a wooden box to a museum; candle slowly goes out. Text: "1943, DONEGAL", then "TAK ADA LAGI YANG MEMBUAT"; small text: "Cetakan gips lentera itu kini dipajang di National Museum of Ireland". Audio: music drops, candle blown out at the end.
5. Resolusi 0:18-0:27 — red dot jumps Ireland -> America across the Atlantic, dashed line and ship icon; match cut turnip face -> glowing pumpkin in the same position; split screen turnip | pumpkin. Text: "IRLANDIA -> AMERIKA", "LOBAK -> LABU", big "CUMA PINDAH BENUA & GANTI SAYUR". Audio: music rises, whoosh on the map, pop on turnip->pumpkin, ~0.3 s pause before "ganti sayur".
6. CTA 0:27-0:30 — split screen, both stare at camera; last second cuts to the same turnip close-up as the opening frame. Text: "KIRIM KE TEMAN PALING PENAKUT", small "@titikheran, Fakta 30 Detik". Audio: short sting (not a scream) on the cut.
Last frame = first frame so the video loops.

## Verified facts (the only claims allowed on screen)
- Ireland: turnips carved into scary-faced lanterns with a candle, used on 31 Oct (National Museum of Ireland)
- 19th to early 20th c. turnip carving more common than pumpkins in Ireland / Celtic lands; potatoes, radish, beet also used (Smithsonian)
- 1943: teacher Rois Ní Bhraonáin from near Fintown, Donegal gave a turnip lantern to the museum; type made there around 1900 (NMI)
- She gave it because nobody made that kind any more (Smithsonian, curator quote 2017)
- The original nearly rotted; a plaster cast is displayed at the Country Life museum, County Mayo (Smithsonian, NatGeo)
- Irish immigrants in America found pumpkins much better to carve (NatGeo)
- NOT allowed: "thousands of years old"; any named person or exact year for the crossing to America.
